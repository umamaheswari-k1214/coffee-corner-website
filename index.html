About Us

"For us, coffee isn’t just a product—it’s a story in every cup," says Ananya Rao, founder of a boutique coffee label in Bengaluru. "We work directly with small estate farmers in Chikmagalur, ensuring every bean is handpicked at peak ripeness. The aroma, the body, the subtle chocolate notes—these are not accidents; they’re the result of patience, care, and respect for the land." Across India, brand owners echo this sentiment, emphasizing that their craft is as much about preserving tradition as it is about innovating for a new generation of coffee lovers.
